Not set up Offers and updates go out from your own email or texting app instead: the Crew tab has an Email and a Text button for each person, with the message written in.

To have the platform email crew itself, give it an email account to send from. On Render, add these under your service → Environment:

BACKLINE_SMTP_HOSTThe mail server. Google Workspace or Gmail: smtp.gmail.com. Microsoft 365: smtp.office365.com.
BACKLINE_SMTP_PORT587 (the usual one; leave it out to use 587)
BACKLINE_SMTP_USERNAMEThe email address you send from, such as crew@yourcompany.com
BACKLINE_SMTP_PASSWORDFor Gmail, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, an app password made in that account's security settings, not your normal password
BACKLINE_MAIL_FROMOptional: the name and address crew see, such as Chicago Sound and Backline <crew@yourcompany.com>

Email services such as Postmark or SendGrid work the same way, with the server and login they give you. Replies go to the company email under Company & billing.
