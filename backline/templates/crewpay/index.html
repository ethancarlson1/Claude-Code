How it works

  • Crew are owed once their event is over, and overdue {{ pay_days }} days after that (change this in Settings).
  • Amount due = the flat rate, or hourly rate × hours. After the show, enter actual hours or a final amount on the event's Crew tab.
  • Tick the people you paid, then Mark paid with the date, method and check number.
  • Each crew member's page shows yearly totals for contractor tax forms.
